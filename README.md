☕ Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard
An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze coffee shop sales performance and uncover actionable business insights. This project transforms raw transactional data into dynamic visualizations, helping users monitor revenue, product performance, customer trends, and key business KPIs.

📌 Project Overview
The Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales activity through data cleaning, analysis, Pivot Tables, charts, and interactive slicers.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to:

Track overall sales performance
Analyze revenue trends over time
Identify best-selling products
Monitor peak business hours
Evaluate category-wise performance
Support data-driven business decisions
🎯 Business Objectives
Understand sales patterns and customer purchasing behavior
Identify top-performing products and categories
Measure daily, weekly, and monthly revenue trends
Discover peak transaction hours
Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs)
Improve inventory and marketing strategies
